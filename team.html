Assoc. Prof. Dr. Wudhichai Assawinchaichote

Principal Investigator and Head of Department

Leads the ENE Control Research Group in the Department of Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, King Mongkut’s University of Technology Thonburi, and is Head of the Department. Research interests include control systems and modeling, fuzzy control systems, and control theory and applications.

Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, University of Auckland (2004). M.B.A., Chulalongkorn University (2009). M.S. in Electrical Engineering, The Pennsylvania State University (1997). B.Eng. in Electronic Engineering, Assumption University (1994).
