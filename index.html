RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

Research directions

01

Control Theory

Stability, nonlinear systems, robust control, adaptive control and control-system analysis.

02

Intelligent Control

Fuzzy systems, reinforcement learning, neural control and learning-based adaptation.

03

Robotics

Manipulator control, trajectory tracking, robot dynamics, vision and autonomous systems.

04

Optimization

Metaheuristics, parameter optimization, model-based optimization and controller tuning.

05

System Modelling

Identification, simulation, digital twins and data-driven models of dynamic systems.

06

Networked Control

Cyber-physical systems, event-triggered control, sensing and network-aware control.
